Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name and email address. When you use Expensebook, we also store the expense information you enter, such as amounts, categories, dates and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data only to provide and improve Expensebook: to operate your account, show your expenses and spending summaries, and keep the service secure. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely and kept for as long as your account is active. We take reasonable measures to protect it from unauthorized access, loss or misuse, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure. You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Expensebook uses Google Fonts to display text, which may involve your browser connecting to Google's servers. We do not share your personal or expense data with third parties, except where required by law.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please get in touch with the Expensebook team through the contact details provided on our website.
